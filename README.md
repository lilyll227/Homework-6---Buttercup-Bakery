# Buttercup Bakery
Homework 6

Buttercup Bakery is a cozy baking game where local woodland critters can order various baked goods. Follow the recipe from the first scoop of flour to the final swirl of frosting and try to earn five stars! 

[Play Buttercup Bakery in your browser](https://buttercup-bakery-lily.corkykangaroo.chatgpt.site). No account or installation is required! To play a downloaded copy, extract the project and open `dist/index.html` in a modern browser. Keep the files and folders together.

## How to play
1. Pick a recipe and read the customer's order in the book.
2. Set the measure first, then click and drag the ingredient into the bowl. The measure stays set between pours! Extra ingredients and incorrect amounts affect your rating and outcome. 
3. Stir in circles to whisk the dry and wet ingredients in separate bowls. Pour the dry mixture into the wet bowl and mix them together.
4. Choose a pan. Hold over the spot where you want the batter to land, move to distribute it, and release to stop pouring.
5. Set the oven temperature and timer, then slide the tray inside. You can take it out early.
6. Select a piping bag and drag across the bake to frost it. Pick a topping and click where you want to put it. Serve the order to see your customer's rating and feedback!
